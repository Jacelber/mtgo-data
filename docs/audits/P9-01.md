# P9-01 - Pure Constructed readiness audit and contract plan

Status: `completed_owner_accepted_local_unpublished`

Task type: read-only audit and documentation

Audited base: `c32f197b7584237246a62b756a9f401243086426` (`master`)
Date: 2026-07-30

## Purpose and boundary

This audit turns Phase 9 from a broad roadmap objective into a bounded,
testable sequence for the two pure Constructed event structures:

- `constructed_day2`;
- `constructed_single_stage`.

It inspects configuration, normalized events, opportunity accounting,
statistics, matchup generation, public Schemas, publication, and the deployed
Tabletop consumer. It does not change production code, generated data,
Schemas, statistical formulas, source configuration, event admission, or
front-end behavior. It does not authorize a new event, a live fetch, a
workflow dispatch, or remote publication.

The retained mixed Modern reference event `434455` is the regression oracle.
Any later structure generalization must reproduce its committed normalized,
opportunity, statistics, matchup, catalog, and browser-visible behavior
without changing its public bytes unless an explicitly approved compatible
version migration says otherwise.

## Approved statistical contract already available

No new statistical rule is proposed by this audit. The existing specification
already distinguishes the required modes.

| Structure | Valid primary scopes | Headline advancement/performance treatment | Must not appear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `constructed_day2` | `day1`, `day2`, `all_constructed` | Initial field; Day 1 APPR; Day 2 field count/share/conversion; Day 2 APPR; all-Constructed Swiss record. Day 2 membership is based on the same Constructed event. | A mixed-event Draft-selection warning; a high-score metric substituted for Day 2 conversion. |
| `constructed_single_stage` | `all_constructed` | Initial field; APPR; deterministic high-score count/share/conversion; all-Constructed Swiss record. | Day 1/Day 2 tabs, Day 2 participation/conversion, or a fictional cut. |
| `mixed` (unchanged baseline) | `day1`, `day2`, `all_constructed` | Constructed-only Day 1 and Day 2 metrics; stage-specific high score; combined raw-count record. | Draft in Constructed metrics; Day 2 conversion presented as pure deck performance; a combined high-score result. |

For every structure, ordinary drops retain scheduled Constructed rounds in the
APPR denominator; byes and intentional draws preserve their specified points
treatment but do not enter played-match or matchup samples; awarded wins and
disqualified-participant matches remain excluded according to
`STATISTICS_SPEC.md`. Primary win rate remains literal valid-match
`wins / (wins + losses + draws)`, including real mirror records. The existing
mixed output's legacy `draw_weight` metadata must not be reused to reinterpret
that rate during generalization; it is a compatibility field to be reviewed in
the output-contract task, not a Phase 9 formula change.

## Required strategy boundary

The smallest suitable implementation is one structure dispatcher at the
opportunity-to-output boundary, with a narrow strategy for each explicit
structure. It must share validation, result eligibility, hierarchy rollup,
quality accounting, byte serialization, and publication mechanics. It must
not create a speculative generic event engine or alter collection and
normalization merely to remove the word `mixed`.

```text
reviewed normalized event + classification
                |
                v
     structure-selected opportunity strategy
                |
                v
      structure-selected overview/deck strategy
                |
                +--> shared hierarchical matchup rollup
                +--> shared quality/publication/catalog packaging
                +--> structure-declared public scope contract
```

The dispatcher must reject an undeclared structure, a scope not supported by
that structure, a phase that conflicts with the selected structure, and a
ledger/output identity mismatch. It must not infer a Day 2 cut from round
numbers or synthesize a high-score population when rounds are unavailable.

## Phase 10 overlap

The former Phase 10 roadmap describes mixed-event support, but its substantive
contract is already implemented and accepted through Phase 7 and Phase 8 for

## Planned task sequence

1. `P9-01` (this task): audit the three-layer contract, record mixed-only
   guards and consumers, and freeze the implementation order.
2. `P9-02`: create deterministic synthetic fixtures and Schema/output-contract
   cases for both pure structures. Confirm exact scope availability,
   unavailable states, and byte-stability expectations before changing a
   generator.
3. `P9-03`: generalize deterministic opportunity accounting through explicit
   structure dispatch. Retain the `434455` ledger as a byte-identical mixed
   regression fixture.
4. `P9-04`: implement structure-specific overview and deck statistics from the
   ledger, including pure Day 2 conversion and single-stage high-score output.
5. `P9-05`: generalize matchup, quality, publication, catalog, and versioned
   public Schemas to consume only each structure's declared scopes.
6. `P9-06`: make the Tabletop consumer structure-aware: Day 2 events retain
   three scopes, single-stage events expose only all Constructed Swiss, and
   mixed selection-bias context remains mixed-only.
7. `P9-07`: after separate owner selection of one candidate event per mode,
   perform bounded live-source smoke validation. Whitelist activation,
   retained raw input, generated data, and publication require separate
   authorization.
8. `P9-08`: run cross-structure regression, public-schema and browser
   verification, then complete Phase 9 closeout if both approved modes are
   evidenced.

## P9-02 acceptance contract

P9-02 must not contact Melee or alter `configs/melee_events.yaml`. It is ready
only when deterministic fixtures establish all of the following:

- `constructed_day2` includes eligible starters in Day 1, evidence-based Day
  2 participants only in Day 2, and no theoretical Day 2 rounds for
  non-qualifiers;
- `constructed_single_stage` exposes exactly `all_constructed`, uses the
  specified high-score threshold, and has explicit null/unavailable values
  rather than hidden or zero Day 2 fields;
- ordinary drops, byes, intentional draws, Top 8 locks, awarded wins, normal
  draws, and disqualifications follow existing shared rules where applicable;
- parent/subtype conservation, real mirror W-L-D, source separation, and
  deterministic serialization are proven for both structures;
- the unchanged mixed `434455` fixture reproduces byte-identically through the
  same public entry points.

## Single-event and multi-event scope selection

The owner approved the following conservative cross-structure consumer
contract on 2026-07-30:

| Selection | `mixed` | `constructed_day2` | `constructed_single_stage` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One event | `day1`, `day2`, `all_constructed` | `day1`, `day2`, `all_constructed` | `all_constructed` only |
| Two or more events | `all_constructed` only | `all_constructed` only | `all_constructed` only |

Multiple selection remains restricted to compatible approved events of the
same Constructed format and source product. Its matrix is recalculated from
the selected events' underlying valid W-L-D counts. It is not an average of
event rates and it does not merge overview metrics.

For one single-stage event, Day 1 and Day 2 do not exist and are omitted. For
multiple events, stage scopes may exist on individual events but are disabled
by the initial product policy; keeping their controls visible and greyed out
with an explanatory message distinguishes this from absent data. Selecting a
second event while Day 1 or Day 2 is active switches to `all_constructed`.
Returning to one event may restore that event's last single-event scope only
when the catalog still declares it supported.

P9-02 freezes these states in the event/public contract. P9-06 owns the
single-event selection behavior. Phase 11 still owns the actual multi-event
raw-count aggregator and its compatibility checks. This division avoids
pulling Phase 11 computation into Phase 9 while preventing a later consumer
from inventing cross-structure scope behavior.
