## Purpose and boundary

P8-07 is the mandatory data-backed owner gate before the production front-end
split. It loads retained Standard, Modern, and Tabletop production payloads
into an independent review harness so that density, wording, responsive
behavior, unavailable states, and hierarchy interactions can be reviewed
before P8-08 begins.

The review harness is under `docs/prototypes/P8-07/`. It directly fetches
governed `stats/` paths from `stats/catalog.json`; it contains no copied
tournament statistics or invented events. It reuses the accepted P8-02 visual
baseline but does not modify that historical prototype.

This task changes no production page, root `index.html`, production asset,
generator, classifier, statistical formula, Schema, workflow, public JSON, or
retained source data. P8-08 remains unauthorized until owner acceptance.

## Resolved bridge blockers

The original P8-07 audit found four backend blockers. The separately published
`P8-07-BACKEND-CONSUMER-BRIDGE` resolved each one:

1. generated subtype labels are complete and self-contained;
2. Tabletop records include literal `wins_over_valid_matches` values while
   preserving compatibility fields;
3. every retained Top 8 week references one immutable same-week construction
   base and publishes exact-deck deviation or an explicit unavailable state;
4. `stats/catalog.json` publishes format/product availability and paths.

The real-data review performs no percentage averaging. Matchup expansion rolls
up only the canonical backend W-L-D leaf counts for the selected row and
column identities, then applies the frozen literal formula. Parent archetypes
remain visible while either axis expands.

## Owner real-data review revision

The 2026-07-28 real-data walkthrough produced a presentation-only revision
inside P8-07. It does not change a generator or statistical contract:

- the Chinese product label is `MTGO占比统计`; the MTGO official source is
  explained in the source note instead of the product name;
- the unpublished 36-week option is hidden until that retained range is
  complete;
- the MTGO distribution summary uses separate high-score-share and
  Top-8-share pie charts. Every parent above two percent receives its own
  segment and the remaining long tail is grouped as `其他`. Each SVG segment
  is keyboard-focusable and reveals name, share, and source deck count on
  hover or focus; clicking pins the same readout;
- the MTGO source explanation reuses the current production wording about the
  public Top-32 boundary, the resulting whole-metagame bias, and why the
  product emphasizes high-score deck counts;
- visible hierarchy copy uses `类型` and `子类型`, and the suspended expand
  control no longer overlaps the aligned identity text;
- maintained subtypes with no data in the active range or event scope are
  omitted from MTGO distribution, both matchup matrices, and Tabletop
  overview. Parents remain present; expansion eligibility is recalculated
  from the active children, preventing historical rules from producing empty
  child rows;
- a second click on a selected construction identity closes the inline detail
  in the MTGO distribution, weekly Top 8, and Tabletop overview products;
- displayed per-card construction differences follow the production
  `DIFF_MIN = 1` threshold. The earlier recollection of a 0.5-card threshold
  was not the production implementation;
- both matchup products use 56-pixel cells, continuous red-yellow-green
  interpolation, real W-L-D hover details, and retained-parent expansion.
  The whole parent label is now an expand/collapse target, while the larger
  row control is suspended at a fixed left position and vertically centered.
  Every row identity therefore starts at one text edge. Column controls are
  suspended in the top-left without consuming label height; expandable and
  non-expandable vertical labels share one bottom baseline and horizontal
  offset, and full names are not ellipsized;
- the revised prototype proposes one shared low-sample warning threshold:
  fewer than 20 valid matches. A 20-match 50% estimate still has an
  approximately 20-point Wilson 95% half-width, so the legend explicitly says
  that this is a warning line rather than a reliability guarantee. The
  Tabletop backend contract remains unchanged until the owner confirms the
  proposal;
- the Tabletop event selector label is `选择赛事` in both subviews; its overall
  row remains fixed while parent rows can be sorted by every meaningful
  metric;
- a selectable Tabletop identity opens the best submitted event deck for the
  active scope. Selection excludes disqualified records and orders by
  scope-specific average constructed points per effective round, then
  constructed points, then wins. It never uses mixed-event overall points or
  final rank as a performance metric;
- that Tabletop detail reuses the two-column construction UI: the left side is
  the selected real event deck, and the right side is explicitly labeled as
  the MTGO recent-four-week average/typical reference. No cross-product
  deviation is synthesized in the browser;
- the best-deck heading is user-facing and no longer repeats event/subtype
  context. The raw `GetDecklistDetails` JSON endpoint is not presented as a
  human deck-page link because the retained contract has no verified Melee
  view-page URL;
- Tabletop score, win rate, valid-match count, and completion rate have
  restored definitions in header tooltips;
- generated recent-change reasons are translated for users: missing four-week
  construction base, insufficient recent-week sample, and insufficient prior
  sample are shown as explanatory Chinese states rather than `nobase`,
  `recent`, or `prior`;
- the redundant four-number Tabletop summary and Unknown/classification
  quality prose are removed. Disqualification treatment and the mixed-event
  Day-2 selection-bias notice remain once, inside a normally indented quality
  section.
