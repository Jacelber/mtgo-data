priority, all matched rules with condition evidence, top-priority matches, and
lower-priority overridden matches. Unknown records retain normalized main-deck
and sideboard card evidence. Conflicts and invalid inputs would remain visible
and block strict generation.

`eldrazi-tron/colorless`. This does not restore statistical eligibility:
