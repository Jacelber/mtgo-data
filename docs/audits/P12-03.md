## Four-week movement trial

The owner selected the aggregated previous four complete weeks as the movement
baseline and the previous complete week as a displayed reference only. The
four-week share divides aggregated archetype high-score count by aggregated
total high-score count; it is not an average of weekly shares.

Eligible `share_move` counts require the current or four-week archetype share to
reach the 3% environment boundary and both populations to contain the known
archetype. Counts below show absolute movement thresholds.

| Threshold | Standard min / median / max | Empty weeks | Modern min / median / max | Empty weeks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2pp | 2 / 7 / 9 | 0 | 3 / 6 / 10 | 0 |
| 5pp | 1 / 3 / 5 | 0 | 0 / 0 / 2 | 9 |
| 7.5pp | 0 / 1.5 / 2 | 2 | 0 / 0 / 1 | 10 |
| 10pp | 0 / 0 / 2 | 8 | 0 / 0 / 0 | 12 |

The owner accepted five percentage points as the first production threshold.
It is strict for Modern and must not be lowered to fill a layout. The current
classifier shadow found one known 5% return, Standard Sultai Reanimator in
2026-W31, and one 5% exit, Standard Mardu Discard in 2026-W24. Modern had no
return or exit at that boundary. Those rare states do not justify a separate
public return type.

## New deck contract

The statistical `new_entry` concept is removed. A Landing new deck exists only
when Weekly Pickup identifies a new parent against maintained known state and a
human approves the item. The retained candidate files available for Standard
2026-W28 through W31 and Modern 2026-W29 through W31 contained no new-archetype
candidate. A known return remains a share movement, not a new deck.

## Subtype build-shift trial

The revised candidate unit is one maintained subtype per week, represented by
its most-deviant concrete current-week Top 8 main deck against the previous four
weeks of the same subtype. The reference minimum is eight decks and one Top 8
result is sufficient.

| Deviation threshold | Standard median / max | Standard empty weeks | Modern median / range | Modern empty weeks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 0 / 1 | 10 | 10 / 8-11 | 0 |
| 15 | 0 / 1 | 10 | 8 / 5-9 | 0 |
| 20 | 0 / 1 | 10 | 4.5 / 2-8 | 0 |

The owner accepted a score of at least 20. Standard scarcity reflects its
current sparse maintained subtype taxonomy and is not filled through parent
fallback.

## Accepted editorial and failure contract

- Public observations contain no separate `notable` flag and no statistical
  `new_entry`.
- Landing renders at most five truthful observations and may render fewer.
- New-deck and new-technology features come from at most two approved Weekly
  Pickup items.
- The generator suggests cards newly present or increased against the relevant
  four-week construction reference; a human chooses the final eight cards.
- Two environment representative cards are manual metadata outside classifier
  rules. Subtype configuration wins, parent fallback must be explicit, and no
  mapping yields readable text-only output.
- No events and no approved feature are distinct Schema-valid states. Invalid
  required data blocks publication. External-image failure does not.
- A late event that changes reviewed facts triggers re-review and preserves the
  last admitted Landing rather than mixing refreshed facts with stale copy.
- The explicit pre-closeout product URL uses `product=mtgo-landing`; the bare
  MTGO default changes only at P12-16.
- Mobile presentation retains names, current/previous/four-week values,
  direction, feature category, and navigation even when images are deferred or
  replaced.

## Atomic format launch and migration exception

Every future public MTGO format must admit Landing and all required products in
one launch. A partial public catalog state is invalid and publication-blocking.
Standard and Modern are the explicit migration exceptions because their
products already exist; they remain online during Phase 12 and satisfy the
same rule at P12-16. Runtime failure of an admitted Landing still offers retry
and a route to statistics.

## Classifier gate

thresholds re-confirmed, and representative cards approved. P12-03 does not
authorize that remediation or P12-10.
