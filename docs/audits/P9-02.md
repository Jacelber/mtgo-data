## Frozen structure contract

| Structure | Supported scopes | Default | Primary advancement metric |

| `constructed_day2` | `day1`, `day2`, `all_constructed` | `all_constructed` | `day2_conversion` |

The pure Day 2 fixture contains four starters and two participants with
explicit Day 2 evidence. Day 1 therefore has 12 theoretical rounds, Day 2 has
four, and the combined scope has 16. One verified Top 8 lock exemption reduces
Day 2 effective rounds to three and combined effective rounds to 15. The two
non-qualifiers receive no Day 2 rounds. An ordinary Day 2 drop retains both
scheduled Day 2 rounds.

The single-stage fixture contains four players and five scheduled rounds each.
The deterministic threshold is
`3 * (floor(5 / 2) + 1) = 9`; two players reach it. The scope has 20
theoretical rounds even though one player drops. Day 2 conversion is explicitly
unavailable with reason `no_day2_cut`; no Day 1 or Day 2 scope is emitted.

Both fixtures retain additive parent/subtype examples. Initial, Day 2, and
high-score subtype counts reconcile exactly to their parent counts.

## Shared result and rate contract

The fixture preserves the established result rules:

- played wins, losses, and normal draws enter literal win-rate and matchup
  samples;
- normal draws contribute one Constructed point, zero wins, and one valid
  match to the denominator;
- intentional draws and byes may contribute points but do not enter win-rate
  or matchup samples;
- ordinary unplayed drop rounds remain effective theoretical rounds;
- a verified Top 8 lock award contributes no points or match and removes its
  effective theoretical round;
- disqualified source records remain retained while the complete affected
  match is excluded symmetrically;
- playoffs remain outside primary Swiss performance.

The multi-event formula in `STATISTICS_SPEC.md` is reconciled to the already
accepted literal method:

```text
combined wins / (combined wins + combined losses + combined normal draws)
```

The fixture deliberately uses unequal event sample sizes to prove that the
combined value comes from summed W-L-D counts rather than an average of event
percentages.

## Selection contract

One event exposes only the scopes declared by its structure. Two or more
compatible same-source, same-format events force `all_constructed`.
Selecting a second event switches any active Day 1 or Day 2 scope to
`all_constructed`; stage controls are disabled with an explanation during
multi-selection. A single-stage event omits its nonexistent stage scopes.
Event overview statistics remain per-event.

P9-02 freezes this target evidence but does not implement multi-event
aggregation. Phase 11 still owns the raw-count aggregator and compatibility
checks.
