## Purpose and boundary

P9-03 converts the mixed-only Constructed-opportunity generator into the
smallest explicit dispatcher for the three configured Tabletop event
structures:

- `mixed`;
- `constructed_day2`;
- `constructed_single_stage`.

The task changes the opportunity ledger, its Schema, executable contract
tests, architecture documentation, and status record. It does not contact
Melee, admit an event, change the whitelist, retain source data, write
generated output, calculate overview or deck statistics, change matchup or
catalog documents, alter the front end, or dispatch a workflow.

## Structure contract

| Structure | Accepted Constructed stages | Emitted scopes | Population rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mixed` | `day1`, `day2` | `day1`, `day2`, `all_constructed` | Existing Day 1 field and evidenced Day 2 Swiss field |
| `constructed_day2` | `day1`, `day2` | `day1`, `day2`, `all_constructed` | Initial field and actual Day 2 Constructed Swiss participants |
| `constructed_single_stage` | `day1` or `other` | `all_constructed` only | Initial field; no fictional cut or stage scope |

Pure structures reject Draft rounds. A single-stage event rejects a Day 2
round. Mixed and pure Day 2 events reject a cross-structure Constructed stage.
An undeclared structure fails closed.

The dispatcher changes only structure-dependent population and scope
selection. It reuses the existing classification identity checks, source
result handling, scheduled-round accounting, drop behavior, Top 8-lock
exemption, disqualification symmetry, exclusion reasons, ordering, and
canonical byte serialization.

## Frozen result behavior

The P9-02 target fixture now drives executable normalized-event tests:

- pure Day 2 has 12 Day 1 theoretical rounds, four Day 2 theoretical rounds,
  one Top 8-lock exemption, and 15 combined effective rounds;
- its two non-qualifiers receive no Day 2 opportunities;
- the qualified participant who drops retains both scheduled Day 2 rounds;
- single-stage has four starters, five rounds each, 20 theoretical rounds, and
  participant point totals of 12, 9, 6, and 3;
- every single-stage opportunity uses `all_constructed`, and no Day 1 or Day 2
  summary is emitted.

Normal wins, losses, and draws remain eligible according to their normalized
source flags. Intentional draws and byes retain points without becoming
played-match samples. Ordinary missing drop rounds retain effective
theoretical opportunities. Reviewed Top 8 locks do not. Matches involving a
disqualified participant remain retained and symmetrically excluded.

## Schema boundary

`melee-opportunity-ledger.schema.json` remains at compatible version `1.0.0`.
It now accepts all three structures and conditionally requires their exact
scope shapes:

- single-stage requires only `all_constructed` and forbids `day1` and `day2`;
- mixed and pure Day 2 require all three scopes;
- single-stage participant stage flags are both false;
- opportunity rows may use `all_constructed`, while round evidence may retain
  the normalized `other` stage.

P9-03 generalizes only the opportunity-ledger Schema. Overview, decks,
matchup, quality, meta, and catalog Schemas remain fixed to `mixed` until their
approved Phase 9 tasks.
