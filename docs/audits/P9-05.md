## Purpose and boundary

P9-05 completes the event-level backend package for the two pure Constructed
structures introduced in P9-03 and P9-04. It generalizes quality
reconciliation, hierarchical matchup generation, deterministic publication,
format catalog entries, and the four remaining public Schemas.

The task does not admit or fetch a real event, change the event whitelist,
write generated production data, dispatch a workflow, alter MTGO output, or
change the front end. Multi-event raw-count aggregation remains Phase 11 work.
P9-06 remains responsible for consuming structure-declared scopes in the
Tabletop UI.

## Structure-aware complete package

| Structure | Quality reconciliation | Matchup and public scopes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mixed` | existing Day 1 + Day 2 conservation and mixed selection warning | `day1`, `day2`, `all_constructed` |
| `constructed_day2` | Day 1 + Day 2 conservation without mixed selection warning | `day1`, `day2`, `all_constructed` |
| `constructed_single_stage` | one exact combined-scope contract; no fictional Day 2 count | `all_constructed` |

The full `build_event_statistics` and path/CLI boundary now accepts all three
structures. Common coverage, eligible-match, exclusion, Unknown, and
disqualification checks remain shared. Structure-specific conservation is
applied only where the corresponding stage exists.

Matchup generation uses the overview's validated structure contract. The
overview scope order, opportunity-ledger structure, and ledger scope summaries
must agree exactly. Single-stage rounds may be labeled `day1` or `other` in the
normalized event, but their ledger and matchup scope is only
`all_constructed`. No empty Day 1 or Day 2 matrix is invented.

## Publication and quality status

`quality.json` keeps its established producer states:

- `ready` when no issue is present;
- `warning` when one or more non-blocking issues are present.

The public `meta.json` and format catalog use the existing consumer states:

- internal `ready` maps to public `pass`;
- internal `warning` remains public `warning`.

This is a presentation-boundary translation, not a statistical
reclassification. Publication still byte-compares overview, decks, matchup,
and quality against deterministic rebuilds, verifies their common identity and
input provenance, and requires the whitelist format and structure to match the
rebuilt overview.

## Schema boundary

The compatible `1.0.0` matchup, quality, meta, and catalog Schemas now accept
`mixed`, `constructed_day2`, and `constructed_single_stage`. Conditional
contracts require:

- all three scopes for mixed and pure Day 2 documents;
- only `all_constructed` for single-stage documents;
- the same exact scope order in matchup, meta, and catalog;
- existing public `pass` or `warning` quality status in meta and catalog.

No retained public document changed shape, no manifest mapping changed, and no
breaking Schema version increment was required.

## Fail-closed contracts

Tests prove that:

- pure Day 2 quality does not inherit the mixed Draft-selection issue;
- single-stage quality omits the inapplicable Day 2 count;
- pure matchup matrices expose only structure-supported scopes;
- an overview scope order that disagrees with its structure is rejected;
- clean `ready` quality is publication-eligible and maps to `pass`;
- a single-stage meta and catalog validate while exposing only
  `all_constructed`.
