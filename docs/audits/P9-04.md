## Purpose and boundary

P9-04 consumes the structure-dispatched opportunity ledger completed in
P9-03 and generalizes only the deterministic per-event `overview` and `decks`
statistics. It does not admit or fetch a real event, write generated data,
change a whitelist, dispatch a workflow, alter the front end, or generalize
matchup, quality, meta, catalog, or publication behavior.

The existing three-document `build_event_statistics` path and CLI remain
mixed-only because they also produce `quality.json`. The new
`build_event_overview_and_decks` boundary supports all three structures
without prematurely implementing P9-05.

## Structure dispatch

| Structure | Emitted scopes | Advancement metric | High-score behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mixed` | `day1`, `day2`, `all_constructed` | existing mixed contract | Day 1 and Day 2 only |
| `constructed_day2` | `day1`, `day2`, `all_constructed` | `day2_conversion` | unavailable |
| `constructed_single_stage` | `all_constructed` | `high_score_conversion` | available in combined scope |

Pure Day 2 conversion is calculated from evidenced populations:

```text
day2_conversion = Day 2 deck count / initial deck count
```

It is emitted for the overall Day 2 scope and each parent/subtype row. A
zero-observation maintained subtype retains an unavailable (`null`) ratio
rather than inventing a denominator. Pure structures do not inherit the mixed
Draft-selection warning. Single-stage events omit nonexistent stage scopes
instead of emitting empty placeholders.

## Shared statistical behavior

The task reuses the P9-03 opportunity rows and preserves:

- normal result, intentional draw, bye, drop, Top 8 lock, and disqualification
  handling;
- per-player effective-round high-score thresholds;
- parent, subtype, and Unknown identities;
- legacy draw-adjusted record fields as compatibility data;
- nested literal W-L-D records using `wins_over_valid_matches`;
- deterministic input provenance and ordering.

Runtime and executable tests require parent/subtype conservation for deck
counts, points, theoretical rounds, effective theoretical rounds, completed
or exempt rounds, played match participations, all-match W-L-D counts, and
available high-score counts. Rates are recalculated and are never summed.

## Schema boundary

The compatible `1.0.0` overview, decks, and shared-statistics Schemas now
accept all three structures. Structure conditionals require:

- all three scopes for mixed and pure Day 2;
- only `all_constructed` for single-stage;
- `day2_conversion` as the pure Day 2 advancement metric;
- `high_score_conversion` as the single-stage advancement metric;
- overall standing context flagged as non-Constructed only for mixed.

Matchup, quality, meta, and catalog Schemas remain fixed to `mixed`. No public
manifest mapping or retained generated file changed.
