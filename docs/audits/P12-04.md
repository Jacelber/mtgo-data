## P12-04A purpose and authorization boundary

P12-04A is the owner-authorized visual comparison stage. It records the
current rendered baseline and supplies two local, self-contained Landing
directions for owner selection. It does not freeze the durable design-system
authority, modify production HTML, CSS, or JavaScript, or authorize P12-04B or
P12-05.

No repository content was transmitted to Superdesign or another external
design service. The comparison artifact uses no external font, image, script,
or stylesheet request. Every displayed number and editorial claim is marked as
illustrative and is not a generated or reviewed Landing fact.

## Current rendered baseline

The current product was reviewed locally from the exact merged base at:

- Standard MTGO metagame, Chinese, 1-week range, 1440 by 1000 pixels;
- the same Standard view at 390 by 844 pixels; and
- protected Modern Tabletop event `434455`, Chinese overview, 1440 by 1000

problems rather than authorization to change current production behavior in
P12-04A.

## Direction A - Editorial Observatory

Direction A treats Landing as a weekly analytical publication.

- Personality: deliberate, editorial, calm, and MTG-adjacent without imitating
  card-frame ornament.
- Visual language: warm neutral canvas, dark teal authority color, restrained
  ember accent, serif display hierarchy, and sans-serif operational text.
- Information order: weekly conclusion, strongest fact, observations,
  environment structure, then the reviewed feature.
- Density: spacious on Landing, with compact table tokens retained for deep
  statistical views rather than applying editorial spacing to every table.
- Mobile behavior: conclusion and strongest fact appear before the observation
  list; product navigation remains a bounded horizontal control.

Primary risk: without an explicit compact-density tier, the editorial spacing
could make existing matchup and deck tables inefficient. The durable contract
would therefore need separate `editorial`, `standard`, and `compact` density
roles while keeping one typography and color system.

## Direction B - Analytical Console

Direction B treats Landing as the summary screen of a professional analysis
tool.

- Personality: precise, operational, compact, and status-oriented.
- Visual language: navy product shell, cool gray canvas, teal operational
  accent, sans-serif hierarchy, and tabular or monospace numerics.
- Information order: data-health strip, weekly brief and KPIs, change feed,
  environment table, then the reviewed feature.
- Density: naturally close to the existing statistics and Tabletop products.
- Mobile behavior: the shell and status remain visible early, while panels
  collapse to one column and wide tables scroll inside their own container.

Primary risk: Landing may read as another dashboard rather than a distinct
weekly entry point. Editorial review and the distinction between machine facts
and human-selected features are visually less prominent.

## Comparison and recommendation

| Criterion | Direction A | Direction B |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly Landing identity | Strong | Moderate |
| Existing dense-table continuity | Moderate; requires compact role | Strong |
| Human editorial distinction | Strong | Moderate |
| Mobile first-screen clarity | Strong | Strong but control-heavy |
| Migration effort | Moderate | Lower |
| Risk of resembling a generic dashboard | Low | Higher |

Recommendation: select **Direction A** as the governing visual direction. It
best explains why Landing exists as the eventual MTGO entry point rather than
as another statistics tab. P12-04B should still adopt Direction B's tabular
numeric treatment, compact table-density role, explicit data-health strip, and
bounded horizontal containers. Those are implementation patterns inside
Direction A, not a third visual direction.
