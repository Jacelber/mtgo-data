## Special-result boundaries

- Draft Swiss and the Draft Top 8 do not enter the ledger.
- Players without Day 2 participation evidence receive no Day 2 opportunities.
- A missing scheduled opportunity is synthesized only for `dropped` or
  `disqualified`; any unexplained absence fails closed.
- Ordinary drops retain zero-point theoretical opportunities.
- Intentional draws and byes retain their approved Constructed points without
  entering win-rate or matchup samples.
- Verified Top 8 lock awards retain the source value, contribute zero
  Constructed points, and remove one effective theoretical opportunity.
- The disqualified participant remains distinct from an ordinary drop. Both
  sides of all six affected Constructed matches remain present and are
  symmetrically excluded from win-rate and matchup use.
